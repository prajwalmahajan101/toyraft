## Goals

Ship the disk-backed `Storage` implementation that makes process-kill chaos
meaningful:

> fsync-before-respond on every `Append`, atomic `HardState` rename, and
> torn-tail recovery on startup — so a crash mid-write loses at most the last
> in-flight record, never a durably-acknowledged one.

## ADRs introduced

- ADR-0013 — Storage/file on-disk format
  (`docs/adr/0013-storage-file-on-disk-format.md`) — ratifies the segment
  framing (`[len][crc][payload]`, CRC32-Castagnoli, `TRL1` header), the
  tmp+rename+dir-fsync HardState protocol, and — critically — that
  `HardState.Commit` is part of the on-disk wire format (see "What surprised
  us").

## Decisions made (non-ADR)

- **The fault seam is in-process, not real process kill.** `faultVFS` is a
  page-cache fake with durable vs. unsynced buffers and a `Crash()` that drops
  everything not yet fsynced. Real `kill -9` chaos is deferred to Phase 11;
  the in-process fake is sufficient to prove the fsync-ordering invariants
  hold.
- **v1 recovery is tail-only.** `scanSegment` truncates at the first bad
  record (`goodEnd`); middle-record corruption is out of scope for v1
  (deferred — see Follow-ups).

## What surprised us

- **`HardState.Commit` MUST persist — the "commit is in-memory only" rule is a
  node-level policy, not a storage carve-out.** The `storagetest`
  `HardStateRoundtrip` test asserts `Commit:5` survives a reload. The
  ADR-0004/0005 rule that `commitIndex` lives only in memory is a NODE-level
  policy about volatile state; at the STORAGE layer, `Commit` is part of the
  on-disk wire format. This trap was caught in planning/verification (not by a
  failing test in the wild) and is recorded in ADR-0013. Getting it wrong
  would have silently failed conformance in a way easy to misread as a
  storagetest bug.
- **An inherited blocker surfaced from Phase 7 and had to be folded into this
  phase.** `make check-no-time-now` failed on two `time.Now()` calls in
  `internal/raftest/cluster.go:636,655` introduced by the 07-04 raftest rewire
  (commit `5830ac7`) — they slipped through Phase 7 close. Phase 8 files carry
  zero `time.Now()`. The fix (commit `fc11abd`) routed the
  `ProposeIntoForTest` poll bound through a real `clock.Clock`
  (`clock.NewReal()`, the sanctioned wall-clock site), restoring the
  determinism gate. `make verify` (lld-drift + check-no-time-now) now passes
  clean.
